- Collibra

  - Purpose

    - This repository maintains neutral, source-driven support material for Wikipedia-facing work about Collibra as a company and as a platform.

  - Editorial constraints

    - Wikipedia text must be based on reliable published sources.
    - Personal memory can guide research but does not count as a citation.
    - Competing employers, former colleagues, and current employees can all contribute if neutrality is preserved.
    - Private communications stay out of the public article and out of the public repository.

  - Working model

    - Markdown is the canonical source in git.
    - Org and Logseq representations are generated from the canonical Markdown source.
    - Knowledge-graph artifacts, Atom feeds, and RSS feeds are rebuilt whenever the content changes.
    - Pending maintainer notifications are prepared automatically and sent only through explicit opt-in workflows.

  - Initial scope

    - Collibra as a company
    - Collibra Platform as a product and operating model surface
    - public history and ecosystem context
    - notable industry relevance in data governance and data intelligence
